F.R.E.Y.A.

Privacy notice

Last updated 20 November 2026.

FREYA is a private, personal assistant built for and used by one person, Paramvir (Eesah) Bhambra. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What it accesses

  • Bank data, read-only. Account names, balances and transactions from the owner's own accounts, through Lunch Flow (an open banking aggregator). FREYA cannot make payments, move money or change anything at the bank.
  • Google Workspace (calendar, email, Drive), only for the owner's own account and only after he connects it.
  • Notes, habits and prayer logs the owner gives it.
  • Phone awareness (Android app, optional). Each part stays off until the owner switches it on in the app's ⚙ settings:
    • Usage access: how many minutes each app was open, unlocks, use after 23:00 and the current continuous session. Not what he did inside apps.
    • Physical activity: the phone's step counter, for steps per day and how long he has been sitting still. No location trail.
    • Notification access: for each notification, only the app, the time, the system category (for example message or email) and whether and when it was opened. FREYA never reads or stores titles, senders or message text.

How it is used

Only to help the owner: summaries of spending, bill reminders, savings projections, and answers to his questions. Phone awareness totals are used to learn his daily patterns, answer questions like "how much have I been on my phone", and send a few gentle nudges a day (never during salah, private mode, meetings or his quiet hours). No advertising, profiling for third parties, or resale.

Where it is stored

In a private database (Supabase) that only FREYA's server can read, behind the owner's passkey sign-in. Access tokens are stored server-side and never sent to the browser. Phone awareness is uploaded about every 30 minutes as daily totals per app (no content) into server-only tables in the same database.

Who else sees it

Nobody. Data is never sold or shared. The only processors are the services FREYA runs on: Lunch Flow (bank connection), Supabase (storage), Vercel (hosting), and Google's Gemini API, which processes conversation text and merchant names to categorise spending and answer questions.

Keeping and deleting

Data is kept while the owner uses FREYA. He can revoke bank access at any time in Lunch Flow or his bank app, disconnect Google in FREYA, switch phone awareness off in Android settings at any time, and ask for everything to be deleted.

Contact

Paramvir Bhambra, the owner and sole user.
